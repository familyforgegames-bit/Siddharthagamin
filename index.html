About the Forge Family

Family-friendly gaming, made by a real family at home.

The Forge Family is a family of four who play, laugh and learn together. Our channel, Siddharthagamin - Familyforge, is where we share our games, our stories and our everyday fun.

It all started with Lionforge, or Lio. His love of Minecraft began at the age of 5 and grew so big that it pulled the whole family into the blocky world. Little brother Luanforge followed his Kuka. Dad, Fatherforge, added Minecraft to his PUBG and football. Mum, Motherforge, loves her Hay Day farm and joins us in Minecraft now and then for family bonding time.

We keep everything family-friendly, so kids and parents can enjoy it together. Come for the games, stay for the family.
